# Important Securitisation and IFRS 9 Terms

Since you're new to securitisation and financial analytics, here are key terms explained simply:

## Securitisation Terms

- **Securitisation**: Turning loans (like car loans) into investment products by bundling them and selling shares.
- **Asset-Backed Security (ABS)**: The investment product created from securitisation.
- **Pool**: The group of loans bundled together.
- **Tranche**: Slices of the ABS with different risk levels (senior, mezzanine, equity).
- **Originator**: The bank or company that made the original loans.
- **Servicer**: The company that collects payments from borrowers.
- **Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)**: A separate company created to hold the assets and issue the securities.
- **Vintage**: Loans originated in the same time period.
- **Delinquency**: When a borrower is late on payments.
- **Default**: When a borrower stops paying altogether.
- **Loss Given Default (LGD)**: How much money is lost when a loan defaults.
- **Probability of Default (PD)**: Chance that a borrower will default.
- **Exposure at Default (EAD)**: How much is owed when default happens.
- **Recovery Rate**: Percentage of money recovered after default (e.g., from selling the car).

## IFRS 9 Terms

- **IFRS 9**: Accounting standard for financial instruments, focusing on expected credit losses.
- **Expected Credit Loss (ECL)**: The amount expected to be lost from loans due to defaults.
- **Stage 1**: Loans that haven't deteriorated significantly (12-month ECL).
- **Stage 2**: Loans that have deteriorated (lifetime ECL).
- **Stage 3**: Loans with objective evidence of impairment (lifetime ECL, interest on carrying amount).
- **Lifetime ECL**: Expected losses over the entire life of the loan.
- **12-Month ECL**: Expected losses in the next 12 months.
- **Credit Risk**: The risk that a borrower won't pay back.
- **Impairment**: Reduction in value due to credit risk.
- **Forward-Looking Information**: Using economic forecasts to predict losses.
